Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data The Thread Within collects when you use thethreadwithin.store, its free tools, or buy from us, and what we do with it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

The Thread Within is a business based in England, UK. We are the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law. You can contact us at hello@thethreadwithin.store.

What we collect, why, and our legal basis

  • Email list. If you sign up through our form, we collect your first name and email address, plus the date and details of your consent. We use them to send you the Gauge Fixer link, news about our tools and patterns, and occasional updates. Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Purchases. If you buy the Raglan Calculator, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address and confirmation of payment, but never your full card details. We use this to deliver your purchase, give support and keep financial records. Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records.
  • Etsy purchases. If you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy handles your order and its own privacy policy applies. We see only the order information Etsy shares with us.
  • Messages. If you email us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.

Our free tools

The Gauge Fixer, Yarn Estimator, Yarn Swap and the free Raglan Calculator demo run in your browser. The numbers you type in (such as gauge and measurements) are not sent to us or stored.

Cookies and third parties

We use these services, which may process some data about your visit (such as your IP address) on our behalf or their own account:

  • MailerLite, to run our email list.
  • Stripe, to take payments.
  • Google Fonts, to load the fonts on our pages.
  • get.store (our online store platform), GitHub (website hosting) and Cloudflare (website delivery, security and visitor statistics).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use advertising cookies.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised by UK law, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep your data

  • Email list details: until you unsubscribe, after which we keep only enough to make sure we don't email you again.
  • Purchase and payment records: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, usually six years.
  • Support emails: for as long as we need to help you, then they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to: see the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; and give you a copy in a portable format. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. To do any of this, email hello@thethreadwithin.store and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

Our site and email list are not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell email subscribers.

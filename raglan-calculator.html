Top-Down Raglan Calculator

Enter your gauge and finished measurements. The numbers below update live — cast-on counts, raglan increases, underarm stitches, and sleeve shaping, all worked out for you.

  1. Choose cm or inches at the top of the measurements panel. Switching converts whatever you've already entered rather than resetting it, and every label, hint, and pattern instruction updates to match.
  2. Pick a neckline tab in the results panel — Round neck, V-neck, Boat neck, or Off shoulder. This changes the construction method, not just the label.
  3. Fill in gauge and measurements on the left. A couple of fields only appear for certain necklines — a V-neck depth field shows up only on the V-neck tab, for instance — and the hint under "Neck circumference" changes per tab to suggest a sensible range for that style.
  4. Read the pattern on the right: stitch counts up top, a schematic, then step-by-step instructions that update as you tweak any field. Use "Copy pattern as text" to save it.

All four necklines share the same underlying shaping: 4 raglan lines increasing 8 sts per round (1 st each side, every RS row), equal front and back stitch counts (no separate front-neck scoop shaping), and a straight, unshaped body. What differs between tabs is only how the neckline itself is cast on and finished — round neck joins in the round and ribs first; V-neck starts flat at two front edges and joins partway through; boat neck uses a wide, shallow edge; off-shoulder uses a deep fold-over band, ideally with elastic run through it. All measurements and gauge can be worked in cm or inches — toggle at the top of the form — and every figure below updates to match. All figures are rounded to whole stitches/rounds — swatch and re-check gauge before committing to a size.

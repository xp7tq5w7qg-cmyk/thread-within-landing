Yarn Swap

Found a yarn you’d love to use instead? Let’s compare the labels and see what’s worth checking before you begin.

A little help before you cast on from Piyi ❤️

Perhaps the pattern yarn is hard to find, a little outside your budget, or you’ve already got something lovely in your stash. A different yarn might work — but matching the weight on the label is only part of the picture.

I made Yarn Swap to help you compare the two. Add their label details to check length per 100 g, explore general differences in fibre behaviour, and estimate how many balls or skeins would cover the pattern’s total length with a little extra.

If you add both prices in the same currency, you can compare costs too. The replacement quantity includes your chosen buffer, so the cost comparison includes that extra yarn.

The score is a rough comparison of these details, not a guarantee of matching gauge, drape or fit. Yarn construction and fibre blends matter too. Let a washed swatch help you decide whether you like the fabric before committing to the project.

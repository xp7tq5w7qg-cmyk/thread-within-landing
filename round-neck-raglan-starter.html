The front drop is measured from the back-neck starting level before ribbing. Check the opening will pass comfortably over your head once you add the neckband.

  1. Cast on and place markers

  2. Work the raglan and neckline flat

    On a scheduled increase row, work to one stitch before each raglan marker. Make one left (M1L) if the section before the marker is scheduled, knit one, slip the marker, knit one, then make one right (M1R) if the section after the marker is scheduled. If a front section has only one stitch and there’s no strand available, use a backward-loop increase in place of that raglan increase. Front neckline cast-ons are separate from the raglan increases.

  3. Join at centre front
